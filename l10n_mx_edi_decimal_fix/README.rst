Some databases configure the currencies (MXN, USD...) with more than 2
decimals, for instance 6, to keep the exact amounts of the accounting.
The CFDI then prints the amounts with those 6 decimals
(``SubTotal="41753.115000"``) and the PAC rejects the invoices and the
payment complements, because the SAT only accepts the decimals of the
currency catalog (2 for MXN and USD).

This module keeps the accounting with 6 decimals and stamps the CFDI
with the decimals the SAT accepts:

- Invoices and credit notes: ``SubTotal``, ``Descuento``, the document
  taxes and ``Total`` are rounded HALF-UP to 2 decimals, and ``Total``
  is computed from the rounded amounts (local taxes included), so the
  arithmetic the PAC checks holds.
- Payment complements: ``ImpSaldoAnt``, ``ImpPagado``,
  ``ImpSaldoInsoluto``, ``BaseDR`` and ``ImporteDR`` have 2 decimals
  (``ImporteDR`` within the CRP20261 limits), ``BaseP``/``ImporteP`` are
  the sum of the DR amounts divided by ``EquivalenciaDR`` rounded to 6
  decimals without trailing zeros (CRP20268, CRP20274, CRPER654),
  ``Monto`` is built from the rounded ``ImpPagado`` (CRP20217) and the
  ``Totales`` from the P amounts of every ``Pago`` node.

Usage
=====

Nothing to do: once installed, the invoices and payment complements are
stamped with the decimals the SAT accepts, whatever the decimals of the
currency.
